id: macro-solow-steady-state
topic: Growth
front: Solow model: steady-state condition
back: Investment per effective worker equals break-even investment:
$$s\,f(k^*) = (n + g + \delta)\,k^*$$
where $s$ is the saving rate, $n$ population growth, $g$ technology growth and $\delta$ depreciation.

===

id: macro-golden-rule
topic: Growth
front: Golden Rule level of capital
back: The steady state that **maximizes consumption** per effective worker. It satisfies
$$f'(k_{GR}) = n + g + \delta$$

id: macro-euler-equation
topic: Consumption
front: Consumption Euler equation (CRRA utility)
back: $$u'(c_t) = \beta (1 + r_{t+1})\, u'(c_{t+1})$$
With $u(c) = \frac{c^{1-\sigma}}{1-\sigma}$:
$$\frac{c_{t+1}}{c_t} = \left[\beta(1+r_{t+1})\right]^{1/\sigma}$$
**Intuition:** at the optimum, giving up one unit of consumption today and saving it can't raise lifetime utility.
